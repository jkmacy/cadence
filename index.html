Private backups & moving devices

Your schedule is saved in this browser, not in GitHub. Your Pixel and Mac do not sync automatically. To transfer changes, export a backup and import it on the other device. Imports replace the current schedule.

Readable JSON contains all schedule details. Encrypted backups require your passphrase; there is no password recovery.

AI collaboration

Copy a plain-text summary of the visible week and paste it into a conversation only when you choose. The app never sends your schedule to an AI.

Starting from scratch

Everything can be edited or removed. Deleting your local schedule cannot be undone unless you have a backup.

Privacy boundary

No account, tracking scripts, external fonts, or remote databases. Browser storage is not encrypted at rest; anyone with access to your unlocked browser profile could read it. Clearing website data can erase your plan. An update to the public app code could also access data stored in this browser, so only install code you trust.
